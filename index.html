Project overview

Four modules, one connected campus

Each team member studies one part of the campus. Together, the four modules show how a physical action on campus becomes data, a record and finally a decision.

Study approach

How we will study the campus

The same four steps guided every module, so all four parts of the study can be compared.

1

Observe

Walked the campus entrance, roads and Eco Campus area and took photographs.

2

Ask

Checked our questions against the university's official website.

3

Map

Mapped how data could move between devices, network and the UMS / ERP.

4

Suggest

Proposed small, practical improvements based on what we found.
