The physical layer of a smart campus

IoT hardware senses identity, temperature, movement or energy use and turns it into data. Dinesh studied readers, sensors, meters, controllers and gateways, and the path that carries a physical event to an application.

Proposed device data fields

Our proposal
  • Device type: RFID reader, sensor or meter
  • Location: room, building or area
  • Reading / event: measured value or detection
  • Timestamp: when the data was produced
  • Device status: online, disconnected or error

When a device fails

Our proposal
  1. 1

    Check device power and input

  2. 2

    Check local controller communication

  3. 3

    Check gateway transfer

  4. 4

    Confirm the application received the event

Findings and suggestions

From finding to improvement

What we foundWhat we suggest
Each sensor serves a different purposeChoose devices by purpose, power and accuracy
Hardware needs a clear identityKeep a device list with location and last-seen time
Controllers process local inputsDocument interfaces and safe behaviour
Faults can hide in the sensor, controller or gatewayTest the data path one stage at a time
Identity data is sensitiveUse dummy credentials in demos, never real biometrics
Devices require maintenancePlan checks for power, cables and readings
