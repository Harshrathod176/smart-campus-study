About the project

What we studied at Vishwakarma University, why, and how the four modules fit into one smart-campus picture.

Introduction

Visit: 16 Oct 2026

A modern campus runs on two kinds of systems: software that keeps records, such as a University Management System (UMS) or ERP, and physical devices that sense or control things, such as attendance readers, meters and sensors. This project studies how these two sides can work together.

On 16 October 2026 our team visited Vishwakarma University (VU), Kondhwa Budruk, Pune. We observed the campus environment, photographed visible facilities and compared what we saw with the services published on the university's official website, vupune.ac.in. We then mapped how data could move and suggested practical improvements.

Objectives

  1. 1

    Understand what a UMS / ERP does and which campus activities it records.

  2. 2

    Identify the IoT devices used on campus and the data each one produces.

  3. 3

    Study how lighting, AC, water and other facilities are monitored or automated.

  4. 4

    Understand, at a high level, how the network and dashboards connect software and devices.

  5. 5

    Find manual or repeated work and suggest practical improvements.

Scope

In scope

  • Student services published on the VU website
  • Visible devices such as CCTV and smart vending
  • Campus utilities: generator, water, Eco Campus
  • High-level network and dashboard view

Out of scope

  • Passwords and credentials
  • Private student records or biometric data
  • Internal IP addresses or network configuration
  • Any security or vulnerability testing

Methodology

Observe → Ask → Map → Suggest

Our project approach is four simple steps, done by every team member for their own module.

1

Observe

Walked the campus boundary, entrance road and Eco Campus area. Photographed visible devices such as a CCTV camera and a smart vending machine.

→
2

Ask

Checked our questions against the official VU website: student services, forms, ERP notes and the facilities list.

→
3

Map

Each student mapped how data moves in their module, from device to network to record.

→
4

Suggest

Proposed small, practical improvements backed by what we saw and read.

What we collected

Done
  • 6 campus photographs, including a group photo with GPS location and time
  • Notes on visible touchpoints: Eco Campus signage, CCTV camera, smart vending machine
  • Public service information from vupune.ac.in (forms, ERP note, facilities)
  • One data-flow diagram per module

What we could not collect

Limits
  • Screenshots of internal systems (no access to the ERP back end)
  • Device readings, meter data or dashboard numbers
  • Network details. These were kept out of scope on purpose
