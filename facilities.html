Our proposal · Example automation flow

Trigger → Data → Rule → Action → Record → Human control

Dhiraj's proposed smart-classroom model. A listed facility does not mean an IoT system is installed, so this is a design idea, not VU's equipment.

1TriggerClassroom becomes occupied
2DataOccupancy sensor detects people
3RuleTimetable is checked, with a time delay
4ActionLights / AC may activate
5Record / AlertTime-stamped status is recorded
6Human controlWall switch always overrides
Timers Schedules Occupancy sensors Thresholds Alerts with an owner Manual override

From finding to improvement

Dhiraj's findings, each with a small, testable improvement.

FindingSuggested improvementSource
