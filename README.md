# University Management System + IoT: Smart Campus Study

A field-work project developed as part of the Second-Year BCA curriculum at **Jaywantrao Sawant Institute of Management & Research (JSIMR), Pune**.

The project studies how a University Management System can connect with IoT-based campus technologies to support attendance, access control, smart facilities, hardware monitoring, networking, dashboards and administrative decision-making.

## Project Modules

### 1. IoT Integration with UMS / ERP
**Harshvardhan Rathod — BC2047**

Focuses on how IoT-generated data such as biometric or RFID attendance and smart access information can be integrated with a University Management System or ERP.

### 2. IoT Hardware
**Dinesh Choudhary — BC2009**

Studies IoT devices such as sensors, readers, controllers, gateways and other hardware used in a smart campus environment.

### 3. Smart Facilities
**Dhiraj Mourya — BC2038**

Studies smart classrooms, lighting, HVAC, electricity, water, parking, alerts, automation triggers and manual override mechanisms.

### 4. Network Security Analytics
**Aaryan Ghadge — BC2019**

Studies the role of campus networks, monitoring, logging, dashboards, backups and alerts while maintaining a safe and non-intrusive academic scope.

## Field Visit

As part of the project, the team conducted a field visit to **Vishwakarma University, Pune** on **16 October 2026**.

The visit helped the team study real campus facilities and compare them with the proposed smart-campus architecture developed for this project.

## Project Approach

The study follows the approach:

**Observe → Ask → Map → Suggest**

The project combines observations from the field visit, publicly available information, academic study and proposed smart-campus solutions.
