Findings and recommendations

What we found at Vishwakarma University on 16 October 2026, from our campus observations and the university's official website.

Visit completed · 16 October 2026
Findings

What the field visit showed

Grouped into five headings. Each finding names its source and module.

1Seen on visit

What we observed

  • VU branding and "Eco Campus" signage at the Kondhwa Budruk campus.
  • A white dome CCTV camera mounted above a building entrance. (04 Network)
  • A touchscreen smart vending machine with an antenna on top, near a building entrance. (02 IoT, 03 Facilities)
  • Maintained campus roads, buildings and boundary walls shared with VIT Pune (Kondhwa Campus).
2From VU website

What works well

  • Student services are published clearly: academic calendar, circulars and notices, results and VUNET registration. (01 UMS)
  • The bonafide certificate application "is now available on your ERP", a working self-service example. (01 UMS)
  • Campus support listed: Wi-Fi, library, cafeteria, generator facility, rainwater harvesting and wastewater management. (03 Facilities)
  • The library offers Wi-Fi, a Web-OPAC catalogue and "Bio-metric Entry and Exit for library users". (02 IoT, 04 Network)
  • Published IT policies for Wi-Fi use and password management. (04 Network)
3VU website + visit

Manual / repetitive processes

  • Most student requests are still downloadable forms: migration, transfer, character certificate, LOR, duplicate ID card, fee structure and no dues. (01 UMS)
  • The vending machine shows a handwritten refund contact number, so refunds appear to be handled manually by phone. (Seen on visit · 02 IoT)
4VU website + visit

Problems / gaps

  • Student services are spread across separate portals: VUNET, the admission portal, the grievance portal and the ERP. (04 Network)
  • Visitors cannot tell whether devices such as the CCTV camera or the vending machine report to any shared dashboard. (02 IoT, 04 Network)
  • A listed facility (for example rainwater harvesting) does not show whether it is monitored. (03 Facilities)
5Our proposal

Improvement opportunities

  • Move more standard forms into ERP self-service with request tracking.
  • A single student entry point linking VUNET, ERP, notices and grievances.
  • A simple facility issue-reporting process before adding sensors.
  • A device register with location and last-seen time, showing "unavailable" instead of a false zero.

Recommendations

What we suggest

One recommendation per module. Each links a problem to a practical improvement and its expected benefit.

Recommendation 101 UMS / ERP

Expand ERP self-service for student requests

ProblemOnly the bonafide certificate is on the ERP; other requests are downloadable forms.
Suggested improvementMove common forms into the ERP with a request reference and status (submitted, in review, ready).
Expected benefitLess repeated data entry and clear status tracking for students.
Proposed by Harshvardhan Rathod
Recommendation 203 Smart Facilities

Start with a facility issue-reporting dashboard

ProblemFacilities such as the generator and water systems are listed, but their monitoring is not visible.
Suggested improvementA simple reported → checked → closed process by building and room, with alerts assigned to a responsible person.
Expected benefitFaster, accountable maintenance, even before installing sensors.
Proposed by Dhiraj Mourya
Recommendation 304 Network / Dashboard

Role-based views and visible service status

ProblemPublic notices and private student records need different access, and service failures are hard to tell apart.
Suggested improvementSeparate student, faculty and admin views, plus an authorised status dashboard with timestamps and an alert owner.
Expected benefitPrivate data stays private, and staff can quickly tell a denied login from an outage.
Proposed by Aaryan Ghadge
Recommendation 402 IoT Hardware

Keep a device register and test in stages

ProblemFaults can hide in the sensor, controller or gateway, and a dead device can look like a zero reading.
Suggested improvementA device list with ID, location and last-seen time; test the data path one stage at a time.
Expected benefitFaster fault-finding, and readings users can trust.
Proposed by Dinesh Choudhary
Learning outcomes

What each of us learned

01

Harshvardhan Rathod

UMS / ERP Main learning

A UMS / ERP is more than marks and attendance. It must link identity, notices, requests and results. I also learned to keep what I saw separate from what I inferred.

02

Dinesh Choudhary

IoT Hardware Main learning

A successful sensor reading is only the beginning. The controller must label it, the connection must deliver it, and the application must spot stale or missing data.

03

Dhiraj Mourya

Smart Facilities Main learning

Useful automation is about reliability, safety and responsibility. A simple alert with a clear owner helps more than a complex system nobody checks.

04

Aaryan Ghadge

Network / Dashboard Main learning

Public information, student records and operational alerts need different levels of access. Security should be designed in from the beginning, not added later.
